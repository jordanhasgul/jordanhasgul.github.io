## About me

Hey 👋, I'm a software engineer and computer scientist based in London, UK.

I'll use this blog to share my opinions on software engineering (and sometimes on computer science). Whilst I spend my 
days building scalable and fault-tolerant distributed systems, my interests lie in the broader craft of software 
engineering. To me, it is a craft of abstraction - designing models of reality that extract the essence of the problem 
to be solved.
